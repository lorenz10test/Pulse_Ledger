Pulse Ledger - Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Pulse Ledger mobile app does with your information.

Last updated: 24 September 2026

In short

  • There is no account. You never sign up, sign in, or give an email address.
  • Your profile, your ledger and your history stay on your device. There is no cloud sync and no server holding your data.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app contains no analytics or ad SDK of any kind.
  • Health data is read only - never written back to Health Connect or Apple Health, never sent anywhere.
  • The only information that leaves your device automatically is what is needed to process and restore purchases.

1. Who is responsible

Pulse Ledger is developed and published by Lorenz0. For any question about this policy or your data, write to i9ye49hq6@mozmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

All of the following is kept in the app's own private storage area on your phone. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone:

  • The profile you enter during onboarding: first name, age, sex, height, weight, activity level, whether you smoke, resting pulse and target lifespan.
  • Your ledger entries - what you logged, when, the duration and the beats involved.
  • Breathing sessions you complete, with the pulse readings you chose to take before and after them.
  • Workouts read from your health app and turned into ledger entries, and the list of workouts already imported.
  • Experiments you start: the change you chose, the number you watch and the dates. The comparison is computed from the daily history above.
  • Habits you add to the cost converter: the name, the minutes and the rise you chose.
  • Daily history of resting pulse, sleep and steps, used to draw your trends and weekly insight.
  • Your settings: theme, sound, reminder times, and whether you have unlocked PRO.

If you uninstall the app, this is removed with it. You can also erase all of it at any time from Settings, using "Delete all my data".

3. Health and fitness data

If you turn on health sync, the app reads the following from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS):

  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability
  • Sleep (stages and sessions)
  • Steps
  • Workouts (exercise sessions: type, start and end), with the heart-rate samples recorded during them

What we do with it: the values are used inside the app to show your resting pulse, your daily history and your trends. They are stored only on your device.

What we never do: we never write data back to Health Connect or Apple Health, never transmit health data off your device, never share or sell it, and never use it for advertising or any form of profiling.

Health sync is optional. You can refuse it, and you can withdraw permission at any time in Health Connect (Android) or in Settings > Health (iOS). The app keeps working with the values you enter by hand.

4. Camera

The camera is used only for the optional pulse measurement. Frames are analysed in memory as they arrive, to detect the small colour changes in your fingertip that indicate a heartbeat. No photo or video is recorded, saved, or transmitted. Nothing from the camera ever leaves the device, and the permission can be refused or revoked at any time.

5. Purchases

Unlocking PRO is handled by RevenueCat, Inc. together with the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. This is the only part of the app that communicates with a server.

Sent: a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID created on your device; purchase and subscription status; store transaction identifiers; app version, device model and operating system version; and your IP address, used to determine your country for pricing and tax.

Never sent: your name or the profile you entered, any health data, your ledger or your history, and any email address or account identifier.

The anonymous ID is not linked to your identity by us. Payment details are handled entirely by Google or Apple - we never see or receive your card or account details.

The legal basis for this processing (where the GDPR applies) is performance of a contract: it is what makes a purchase work and lets you restore it on a new device.

6. Reminders and notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally by your phone. There is no push server, no device token is registered anywhere, and we cannot send you a message. Turn them off in the app or in your system notification settings.

7. Home screen widget

The Android home screen widget reads its figures from the app's own private storage on the same device. Nothing about the widget is transmitted.

8. Exporting and sharing

The app can export a backup of your data as a JSON file, and can produce an image of your life statement to share. Both happen only when you tap the button, and the file goes wherever you choose to send it. We never receive a copy.

Please note that an exported backup file is not encrypted. It contains your profile, your ledger and your history as readable text. Keep it somewhere you trust. The app warns you about this before it exports.

9. What the app does not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is present in the app.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier, no ad network.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no profiling.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we never have.
  • There is no user account, so there is no user database.

10. Keeping and deleting data

Everything the app stores about you lives on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. To erase it:

  • Settings > Delete all my data inside the app removes your profile, ledger, history and settings immediately; or
  • uninstall the app.

Purchase records held by RevenueCat and by the app stores are separate. To ask for deletion of the purchase record associated with your anonymous ID, write to i9ye49hq6@mozmail.com. Google and Apple keep their own transaction records for as long as their own policies and tax law require, and we cannot delete those on your behalf.

11. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with similar law, you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, and to object to processing.

In practice, almost all of your data never leaves your device, so you already hold it and control it directly: view it in the app, export it with "Export backup" for portability, and erase it with "Delete all my data". For anything related to purchases, write to i9ye49hq6@mozmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Children

Pulse Ledger is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is intended for adults.

13. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage area, which the operating system keeps isolated from other apps. We recommend keeping a screen lock and device encryption enabled. A backup file you export yourself is outside that protection - see section 8.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is shown here and published at https://lorenz10test.github.io/Pulse_Ledger/ with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app.

15. One thing that is not about privacy

Every figure in Pulse Ledger - heartbeats left, pulse age, the cost of a habit - is an estimate for perspective, not a prediction and not a medical result. The app is a wellness tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any condition. Talk to a doctor about anything that concerns you.
