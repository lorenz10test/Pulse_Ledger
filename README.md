# Pulse Ledger

Public documents for the **Pulse Ledger** mobile app (Android and iOS).

## Privacy policy

- Published page: <https://lorenz10test.github.io/Pulse_Ledger/>
- Source: [`index.html`](index.html)

This URL is the one to paste into:

- Google Play Console -> App content -> **Privacy policy**
- Google Play Console -> App content -> **Health apps declaration**
- App Store Connect -> App Privacy -> **Privacy Policy URL**
- The app itself (`ProCatalog.privacyUrl`), which opens it from Settings

## Keeping it true

The policy describes what the app actually does. If any of these change, the policy
has to change with them:

- the health data types read from Health Connect / Apple Health,
- what the camera does with its frames,
- adding any analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK (there are none today),
- adding an account, a server, or cloud sync,
- what RevenueCat receives.
